Questions buyers ask

What is the asking price for 13666 179th Ave NE?

The home is offered at $1,319,000.

How many bedrooms and bathrooms does it have?

4 bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms in 1,930 square feet. The fourth bedroom is a separate den on the main floor that also works as an office or playroom.

How big is the lot?

0.28 acre: a level quarter acre with open lawn, herringbone brick paths, raised beds and mature cedars for privacy.

When was the home built, and what has been updated?

Built in 1982. The kitchen and family room were added in 2012, the home was remodeled in 2019, and heat pump central air conditioning, a new furnace and a new water heater were installed in 2022.

What else comes with the property?

A detached studio with power, a storage shed, a playhouse, and a two car garage with an EV charger. The home has natural gas and is smart home ready.

Where is the home?

At the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in the English Hill neighborhood of Redmond, WA 98052, minutes to Redmond, Woodinville, parks, trails and dining.

Which schools serve the home?

The home is in the Northshore School District: Sunrise Elementary, Timbercrest Junior High and Woodinville High. Buyers should confirm school assignments with the district.

How big are the main rooms?

From the floor plan: family room 20'8" x 21'9", kitchen 10'7" x 12'5", dining area 10'7" x 9'5", living room 16'4" x 14'0", main floor den 14'4" x 8'11", primary bedroom 13'0" x 16'0", two bedrooms of 8'11" x 12'8" and 8'8" x 12'9", and a garage of 19'2" x 21'5". Floor plan measurements are not guaranteed.
